Install on your device

Chrome or Edge: open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone or iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

The app opens in its own window. A connection and your usual login are needed to use your library.

New channels start with automatic downloads off. Enabling it watches for new uploads; older episodes stay manual.

Feeds contain downloaded audio. Adding a channel loads its catalog without downloading its episodes.

Add podcasts and combine feeds

Check sources to create a combined feed. New downloads are included automatically.
